    Where leads come from

    Every source is public. Organisations come from government registers and published tenders; people come from public posts where they asked for help. Nothing is bought, scraped from behind a login, or guessed.

    Reaching out properly

    • Organisations: business email to a company address is allowed in the UK when the consultancy is named, the reason is relevant, and every message offers an easy opt-out.
    • People: contact them only through the site where they posted, as a reply or a message, written to them personally. Do not copy their details elsewhere or add them to a mailing list without their consent.
    • Too late is set only with evidence: a tender closed or awarded, a licence restored, a company dissolved or being struck off, the person saying so, or the evidence being too old to act on (a post over 30 days old, an Expansion Worker business set up over two years ago). Each one links to the record that shows it.
    • Dates on every lead are when the source first published the evidence, not when this app found it. Where a source publishes no date, the lead says so.
    • Three months at most: evidence published more than three months ago is never shown, whatever its source. Leads already marked Contacted or Won stay in those tabs.
    • Coverage is the UK and the Isle of Man.

    About SYRIX

    SYRIX is made by Kabirr Kohli.

    This is a version of SYRIX working inside SYRIX Lead Finder: an agent of SYRIX tuned only for this app, here to answer questions, find leads and make the app easier to use. The main SYRIX runs on Kabirr's own computer, with the strongest models, the full feature set and its v3 core.

    In this app it reads the leads, explains who needs what and why, checks companies on Companies House, and looks up current law and official guidance on GOV.UK, legislation.gov.uk and the ICO.

    It can read the app but never changes it: marking a lead stays with you. Answers about the law are general information, not legal advice.
